How the estimate works: a college/branch is Top tier if its 2024 or 2025 closing rank fell within your chosen window just below your rank (e.g. rank 10,000 with a 3,000 window flags closing ranks 7,000–10,000) — these are shown ahead of everything else as your closest, most realistic matches. Otherwise it's Safe if your rank is comfortably better than its 2025 closing rank, Good chance if you're inside the 2025 closing rank with some margin, and Reach if you're right at or just past it — worth trying since ranks shift year to year. The "2026 est." column is a simple straight-line projection from the 2024→2025 move, not an official prediction. Region (AU / SVU / SW) is the institute's affiliating region, relevant to local-area reservation — it doesn't itself change your eligibility, only which local-area quota you'd fall under. SC 2024 figures reflect the pre-subclassification single SC category, so they aren't directly comparable to the 2025 SC Group I/II/III columns.
